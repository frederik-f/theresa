THERESA
=======

**THE**\ rmal **RE**\ pository **S**\ urrogate **A**\ nalysis


Peak temperature at the surface of a waste canister in drift emplacement over
1000 years: a 3D OpenGeoSys 6 heat-conduction model of the quarter unit cell
(Gmsh mesh, process ``HEAT_CONDUCTION``) and a POD–GP surrogate of it.

Version |release|.

.. important::

   Private hobby project for trying out simulation and surrogate-modelling
   techniques. Geometry, material parameters, decay-heat spectra and the
   design limit are simplified or assumed values, not realistic and unrelated
   to any existing or planned repository. The results are not suitable for
   design, licensing or safety assessments. No warranty.
